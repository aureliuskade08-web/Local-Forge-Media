Where roofing companies lose jobs

  • Roofing is a big decision with a long sales cycle.

    Homeowners look at several contractors over weeks. The company that follows up fastest and looks most credible usually wins.

  • Storm-season spikes, then quiet.

    Demand jumps after weather events and drops off. Without ongoing marketing you rely on the next storm.

  • The website doesn't show your work.

    Homeowners want to see finished roofs in their area. A site with no project gallery asks them to take your word for it.

  • Leads go cold.

    An inspection request that waits a day for a reply is often gone by the time you call back.
