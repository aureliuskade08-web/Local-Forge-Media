Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Core and Premium are month to month. The Starter website is a one-time payment.

Who owns my website and ad account?

You do. Ads run in your own Meta ad account, and your domain and website belong to your business.

On Core, you pay Meta directly for ads, and we recommend starting at $1,000 a month. On Premium, up to $1,000 a month in ad spend is included in your fee. Anything above that is billed at cost.

What do you need from me to get started?

A 30-minute strategy call, photos of your recent jobs, your service area, and access to your Google Business Profile and Facebook page. We handle the rest.

Do you work outside Georgia?

Yes. Everything is done remotely, so we work with roofing, HVAC and plumbing companies anywhere in the U.S.
