How the 5-Minute Intake System works

Four layers sit between your website and your calendar. Every inquiry moves through all four in minutes, day or night.

  1. Capture

    Web forms, intake emails and after-hours voicemails land in one queue. Voicemails are transcribed the moment they're left.

  2. Classify

    Each inquiry is sorted by case type, urgency and location. Anything that needs a person right away gets flagged.

  3. Reply

    A personal first reply goes out from your firm's email address, built from templates your attorneys have approved. It never gives legal advice.

  4. Book

    Every reply includes a link to your consultation calendar. Prospects who don't book get two short follow-ups, and you get a monthly report on response times.

Questions attorneys ask

Does the AI give legal advice?

No. Replies acknowledge the inquiry, ask the questions you'd want answered before a consultation, include your disclaimer and offer a booking link. Your attorneys approve every template before anything goes live.

What about bar advertising and solicitation rules?

Replies go only to people who contacted your firm first, and they use the template language you approve for your state. We charge a flat fee, never a share of legal fees or a price per signed case.

We already use Clio Grow or Lawmatics. Do we need to switch?

No. We build on what you already have. If your CRM has a built-in AI intake feature, we cover the gaps it leaves, which are usually email inquiries, voicemail follow-up and after-hours booking.

Can replies go out in Spanish?

Yes. Replies match the language of the inquiry, using Spanish templates your team approves.

Where does client information go?

Inquiries stay in your systems and your inbox. We don't sell or share data, and we sign a confidentiality agreement before setup.
