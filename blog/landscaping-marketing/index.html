Landscaping marketing: how to win more yards

By Malakai Bubar, Local Forge Media · Updated November 16, 2026

Landscaping is a visual business with a strong seasonal rhythm. People hire based on what your finished work looks like, and the best customers are the ones who stay on a recurring maintenance plan. Your marketing should show the work and make the recurring offer easy to say yes to.

These are the pieces we'd put in place.

Show your work like a portfolio

A gallery of real projects, with before-and-after photos, the type of property and what you did, sells better than any sentence. Organize it by service so a homeowner looking for a patio sees patios.

Use your Google Business Profile as a storefront

Add project photos every week during the season, list each service, set your service area and collect reviews. Photos and recent reviews carry a lot of weight for a visual trade.

Build service pages for design and maintenance

Lawn care, planting, hardscaping, irrigation, lighting, cleanups and design are different searches. Give each one a page that explains the process and how to request a quote. A website designed for home service contractors keeps this organized.

Market ahead of the season

Run design and install offers early, when homeowners are planning, not once the schedule is full. Promote maintenance plans in spring and cleanups in fall. A simple calendar of what you promote each month keeps work steady.

Use Meta ads to sell recurring work

Facebook and Instagram let you show finished yards to homeowners in your service area. Lead with a clear offer, like a free estimate or a seasonal package, and send people to a short form or a calendar link.

Follow up on every estimate

Landscaping quotes often sit for a week or two. A friendly follow-up after a few days, and again later, recovers jobs that would otherwise go quiet. An AI receptionist and chatbot can answer questions and book estimates outside office hours.

Measure what turns into jobs

Track quotes sent, quotes accepted and revenue per source, then spend more on what produces signed work.

Common questions

How do landscapers get more customers?

Show real project photos, keep a complete Google Business Profile, build a page for each service, collect reviews and follow up on every quote. Seasonal Meta ads help fill the calendar.

When should a landscaper start marketing for the season?

Earlier than feels necessary. Start design and install offers while homeowners are planning, and promote maintenance plans just before the growing season.

Are maintenance plans worth marketing?

Yes. Recurring customers give you steadier revenue, so it is worth making the plan a clear, easy offer on your site and in your ads.
