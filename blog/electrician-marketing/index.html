Electrician marketing: how to get more booked jobs

By Malakai Bubar, Local Forge Media · Updated November 16, 2026

Electrical work splits into urgent calls, like a dead outlet or a tripping breaker, and bigger planned jobs, like panel upgrades, EV charger installs and rewiring. Each needs a different kind of marketing, and both depend on the customer trusting you enough to let you into their home.

Here is how we'd build it for an electrical contractor.

Lead with licensing and trust

Homeowners are cautious about electrical work. Put your license, insurance and years in business near the top of your site and your Google Business Profile, with real photos of your crew and finished work. Trust signals do more here than clever copy.

Fill out your Google Business Profile completely

Choose electrician as your primary category, list every service by name, set accurate hours and your service area, and add photos regularly. Ask every happy customer for a review and reply to all of them.

Give each job type its own page

Panel upgrades, EV charger installation, lighting, rewiring, generator hookups and emergency repair are separate searches. A page for each, with what the job involves, what affects the price and how to book, gives you something to rank for. A website built for home service contractors sets this up from the start.

Use ads for the right jobs

Search ads fit urgent problems. Meta ads fit planned work, such as EV chargers, panel upgrades and whole-home lighting, because you can reach homeowners before they start comparing. Keep one offer per ad and send people to a matching page.

Answer every call and message

Electrical leads often contact two or three companies. Reply fast, confirm appointments and follow up on every estimate. An AI receptionist and chatbot can answer common questions and book visits when your office is closed.

Track booked jobs and revenue by source

Count calls, booked jobs and revenue per channel. If a channel brings calls that don't book, fix the page or the offer before raising the budget.

Common questions

How do electricians get more customers?

Complete your Google Business Profile, show your license and insurance, build a page for each service, collect reviews and answer every call quickly. Ads add volume once those basics are in place.

Are Meta ads good for electricians?

They work well for planned jobs like panel upgrades and EV chargers, where you can reach homeowners in your area with a specific offer.

Should electricians have a page for each service?

Yes. People search for specific jobs, and separate pages give you a better chance of showing up for each one.
